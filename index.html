    Why remove your data from brokers?

    Brokers assemble public and licensed records into profiles you never asked for — and sell access to anyone who searches.

    Home address exposure

    Full addresses show up on people-search pages, raising risks around privacy, safety, and unwanted contact.

    Phone and spam risk

    Listed numbers feed robocalls, phishing, and marketing lists you never opted into.

    Family and relatives

    Many profiles include household members, widening the exposure surface beyond you.

    Search-engine visibility

    Broker pages often rank when someone Googles your name — including employers and strangers.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does the free scan actually check?

    It estimates potential matches of your name across 26 people-search, background-check, and B2B data sites. It is not a live crawl of every database. Wave 1 covers 16 people-search brokers with automated opt-outs on paid plans.

    What is Wave 1?

    Wave 1 is the first removal slice: 16 major people-search sites, live as of Aug 21, 2026. Paid plans can queue automated opt-outs for those listings. Wave 2 (background-check) is estimated Nov 15, 2026; Wave 3 (B2B) Jan 31, 2027.

    Can OptlyOuts guarantee removal from every site?

    No service can. Brokers control their own databases. Some require extra verification, may refuse, or re-list later. We focus on submitting and tracking requests as effectively as possible.

    How is this different from larger services?

    OptlyOuts is simple and affordable — a clear free scan, a DIY wiki, and straightforward paid plans. No enterprise-style pricing.

    Do you sell or share my data?

    No. We do not sell personal information. We only use what you provide to run scans and assist with opt-outs. See the Privacy Policy.
