Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Optly (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a product of Awakyn Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Optly website and related services (the “Service”).

Optly is designed to help individuals understand and reduce the visibility of their personal information on data broker and people-search websites. Because of the nature of this Service, we take privacy seriously.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name (when you run a scan or create an account)
  • Email address
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe; we do not store full card details)
  • Any other information you choose to submit through forms or support requests

Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address and general location data
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and interactions with the Service
  • Referral source

Scan-Related Information

When you request a web presence scan, we process the name (and related identifiers you provide) to search publicly available sources. Results may include publicly available personal information found on third-party websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To perform web presence scans and display results
  • To provide opt-out assistance and monitoring services
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To communicate with you about your account or the Service
  • To improve the Service and fix issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers — such as Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting/infrastructure), and email providers, solely to operate the Service
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice where required)

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Scan results and account data may be retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information

  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@awakyn.ai.

8. Third-Party Websites

Optly interacts with third-party data broker and people-search websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

10. International Users
